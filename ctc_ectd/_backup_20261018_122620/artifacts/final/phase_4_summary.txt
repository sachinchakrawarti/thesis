6. Interpretation
--------------------------------------------------------------
  Precision = fraction of fired alerts that were real
  Recall    = fraction of real events that were caught
  F1        = harmonic mean
  Lower threshold -> more alerts, higher recall, lower precision
  Higher threshold -> fewer alerts, higher precision, lower recall

  Ground truth is synthetic; alert calibration on real data
  would require independently labeled incident reports.
