## Synthesis questions

1.  Which classifiers are commonly used for CTI text?
2.  Which text representations are compared?
3.  How do studies define an emerging threat?
4.  Do studies use chronological evaluation or random splits?
5.  Are topic changes validated against incident data?
6.  What limitations recur across studies?
7.  Which gap can the present prototype reasonably address?

## Rules

-   Keep author-reported results separate from your interpretation.
-   Verify title, author order, year, venue, DOI, and page numbers.
-   Do not invent missing metadata.
