# Citation Verification Checklist

For every reference: - \[ \] Opened the full paper or authoritative
publisher record - \[ \] Exact title verified - \[ \] All authors and
author order verified - \[ \] Publication year verified - \[ \]
Journal/conference name verified - \[ \] Volume, issue, pages/article
number verified where applicable - \[ \] DOI or official URL verified -
\[ \] Claim in thesis actually supported by the source - \[ \] Citation
style consistent - \[ \] In-text citation appears in the reference list
and vice versa

## Final thesis citation audit

Search the thesis for `TODO`, `VERIFY`, `FILL`, `placeholder`, and empty
brackets. Remove every unresolved placeholder before submission.
