### 4.12 Performance Evaluation

Use multiple metrics rather than accuracy alone.

**Accuracy**

$$
Accuracy=\frac{TP+TN}{TP+TN+FP+FN}
$$

**Precision**

$$
Precision=\frac{TP}{TP+FP}
$$

**Recall**

$$
Recall=\frac{TP}{TP+FN}
$$

**F1-score**

$$
F1=2\frac{Precision\times Recall}{Precision+Recall}
$$

For a multi-class problem, report:

* Macro Precision
* Macro Recall
* Macro F1
* Weighted F1
* Confusion Matrix

You can also evaluate the explainability component using measures such as explanation consistency, feature attribution stability, and human/analyst assessment if such evaluation is part of your actual experiment.
