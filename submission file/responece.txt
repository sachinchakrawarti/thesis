### Strong research gap

A good thesis gap can be framed as:

> Existing CTI research has demonstrated automated threat classification and topic-based emerging threat detection, but there remains scope for integrating modern contextual language representations with temporal topic evolution to improve the identification of emerging cyber threats from continuously arriving CTI reports.

This gives you a legitimate **M.Tech-level extension** rather than copying the paper.

### Your 5 major research objectives

1. **Develop a CTI text-processing pipeline** for collecting and preprocessing cybersecurity threat reports.

2. **Develop and compare machine-learning and deep-learning models** for automated cyber-threat classification.

3. **Evaluate transformer-based text representations** against conventional TF-IDF and Word2Vec representations.

4. **Model temporal topic evolution** using LDA, NMF and/or BERTopic to identify changes in cybersecurity discussions.

5. **Develop an emerging-threat detection mechanism** based on sudden changes in topic prevalence and threat-related terminology.
